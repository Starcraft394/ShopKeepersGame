# Shops & Shadows -- Game Guide

Welcome to **Shops & Shadows**, a cozy grim-fantasy roguelite where you play as the Shopkeeper -- the quiet architect behind every adventure. You do not fight. You recruit heroes, equip them, send them into corrupted dungeons, and use the resources they bring back to grow your town. Heroes are expendable. Your town is not.

---

## Getting Started

When you first launch the game, it boots into the **Town Hub** -- a bird's-eye view of your starting town, Thornhaven, nestled in the Forest Haven region. The screen is split into a **navigation rail** on the left, a **building grid** in the center, and a **party bar** at the bottom.

On your very first launch you will have no heroes and **400 gold** to spend. The game automatically opens the **Inn** to get you started.

### Your First Steps

1. **Recruit heroes at the Inn.** The Inn shows a roster of available recruits. Each candidate has a race, class, and stat block. Recruit up to 4 heroes to form your starting party.
2. **Equip your heroes.** Visit the General Store or a crafting facility (Blacksmith, Huntsman, Enchanter) to buy or craft starter gear. Equip weapons, armor, and a backpack to each hero.
3. **Enter a dungeon.** Open the Dungeon Board to select a dungeon floor and send your party in.

That is the core loop: recruit, equip, dungeon, loot, repeat.

---

## The Town Hub

The Town Hub is your home base. Every town has a set of facilities you can visit by clicking the building icons in the center grid, or by using the navigation rail on the left side.

### Facilities

| Facility | What It Does |
|----------|-------------|
| **Inn** | Recruit new heroes, manage your roster, dismiss heroes, and manage hero gear and bags via the Manage Gear popup. |
| **General Store** | Buy and sell items. Stock is based on recipes you have unlocked at crafting facilities. |
| **Storage** | View and manage your material stash. Filter by category (materials, consumables, equipment, books). Consumables have a "To Bag" button to send them directly to a hero's bag. |
| **Training Hall** | Use Class Books to learn new classes. Assign learned classes to heroes. Train heroes for a stat boost on their next dungeon run. |
| **Dungeon Board** | Select a dungeon floor and launch a run with your current party. |
| **Blacksmith** | Craft and upgrade melee weapons, shields, and heavy armor. Unlock new recipes by spending materials. |
| **Huntsman** | Craft bows, light armor, and travel gear from pelts and forest materials. |
| **Enchanter** | Craft staves, magical foci, and accessories. |
| **Alchemist** | Mix monster parts and herbs into healing tonics, elixirs, and utility potions. Supports discovery-based mixing. |
| **Chef** | Combine food ingredients into meals that provide combat buffs. Supports discovery-based mixing. |

### Navigation

- The **left rail** lists all facilities in the current town, plus region travel buttons if you have unlocked additional regions.
- Click any building in the center grid or the nav rail to open that facility's panel.
- The **party bar** at the bottom shows your current hero roster.
- **Save & Exit** saves your progress and closes the game.
- **Options** opens audio and settings.

### Traveling Between Regions

Once you defeat a region's boss, the next region unlocks. Use the region buttons in the nav rail to travel to unlocked regions. Each region has its own town with the same facility types but different crafting recipes, recruits, and dungeon content.

## Heroes

Heroes are the adventurers you send into dungeons. They fight, they loot, and sometimes they die -- permanently. Your job is to keep the pipeline of heroes flowing.

### Races (9 Total)

Heroes come from 9 different races, unlocked as you progress through the regions:

| Region | Race | Notable Trait |
|--------|------|--------------|
| 1 -- Forest Haven (Thornhaven) | **Human** | Balanced stats, learns 10% faster |
| 1 -- Forest Haven (Thornhaven) | **Elf** | Fast and perceptive (+3 SPD, reveals dungeon nodes) |
| 1 -- Forest Haven (Thornhaven) | **Dwarf** | Tough and slow (+10 HP, +3 DEF, -3 SPD) |
| 2 -- The Fungalmire | **Mossfolk** | Regenerates HP each turn |
| 3 -- The Sunken Strand | **Tideling** | Fast, water resistant |
| 4 -- Ashen Horizons | **Dragonkin** | Scales reduce all damage, burn immune |
| 5 -- Starfall Expanse | **Crystalborn** | High DEF, magic damage reduction |
| 6 -- The Necropolis | **Undead** | Poison and bleed immune, high ATK and DEF |
| 7 -- Final Realm | **Voidwalker** | Extremely fast, chance to dodge |

Later races are more powerful but gain experience more slowly.

### Classes (15 Total)

Classes determine a hero's combat role, abilities, and stat growth. There are 4 archetypes:

| Archetype | Role | Classes |
|-----------|------|---------|
| **Vanguard** | Frontline tank / protector | Defender, Pyrewarden, Prism Sentinel, Voidwalker |
| **DPS** | Damage dealer | Striker, Fungal Berserker, Stormcaller, Ashblade, Prism Lancer, Lich, Void Herald |
| **Healer** | Restoration / support | Druid, Tidechaser, Dark Channeler |
| **Warden** | Hybrid support / healing | Warden |

The 3 starter classes (Defender, Warden, Striker) are available from the beginning. Additional classes unlock by finding **Class Books** in dungeons or shops and using them at the Training Hall.

| Region | Classes Unlocked |
|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | Defender, Warden, Striker |
| 2 | Druid, Fungal Berserker |
| 3 | Tidechaser, Stormcaller |
| 4 | Pyrewarden, Ashblade |
| 5 | Prism Sentinel, Prism Lancer |
| 6 | Dark Channeler, Lich |
| 7 | Voidwalker, Void Herald |

### Hero Abilities and Passives

Every hero has:

- **Ability A** -- A primary combat skill (often a direct attack or heal)
- **Ability B** -- A special skill with a longer cooldown (AoE, buff, debuff)
- **Passive A** -- An always-on or triggered bonus from their class
- **Passive B** -- A second passive bonus from their class
- **Racial Passive** -- A bonus from their race

### Equipment Slots

Each hero can equip items in 8 slots:

| Slot | Examples |
|------|---------|
| Weapon | Swords, bows, staves, maces, axes, daggers |
| Offhand | Shields (DEF/HP), magical foci (ATK/HP) |
| Helmet | Light hoods, medium coifs, heavy helms |
| Chest | Robes, vests, chainmail, plate |
| Legs | Leggings, greaves |
| Ring | Stat rings |
| Amulet | Pendants and charms |
| Backpack | Increases personal bag capacity |

### Manage Gear Popup

From the Inn, click **Manage Gear** on a hero card to open a popup that shows:

- All 8 equipment slots with equipped items and Unequip buttons
- The hero's **bag inventory** -- each bag slot shows its contents with a Remove button, and empty slots are displayed
- An **"Add Item to Bag"** button that opens a stash item selector so you can load consumables, materials, or other items directly into the hero's bag from town storage

This is the central place to prepare heroes for dungeon runs.

### Storage "To Bag" Transfers

In the **Storage** screen, consumable items display a **"To Bag"** button. Clicking it opens a hero chooser popup that shows each hero's name and bag capacity (e.g., "3/5 slots used"). Select a hero to send the consumable directly into their bag without navigating to the Inn.

### Party Size

Your party size is **4 heroes** at all Inn tiers. You can recruit and deploy up to 4 heroes from the start of the game.

## Equipment & Items

The game has 430 items across four categories.

### Equipment (284 items)

Equipment provides stat bonuses across **10 combat stats** and scales across 5 tiers:

### Combat Stats

| Stat | Abbrev | What It Does |
|------|--------|-------------|
| **Health** | HP | Maximum hit points. Reaching 0 = death. |
| **Attack** | ATK | Increases damage dealt with abilities and basic attacks. |
| **Defense** | DEF | Reduces physical damage received (soft-capped: full value up to 20, half 21-40, 20% above 40). |
| **Speed** | SPD | Determines turn order; 40+ SPD = 2 actions per round, 80+ = 3 actions. |
| **Crit Chance** | CRIT | Percentage chance to deal 1.5x damage on hit (capped at 50%). |
| **Evasion** | EVA | Percentage chance to completely dodge an incoming attack (capped at 50%). |
| **Resist** | RES | Reduces fire, dark, and void damage (same soft-cap formula as DEF). Does NOT reduce physical or "magical" damage. |
| **Thorns** | THN | Flat damage returned to physical attackers on hit. |
| **Armor Penetration** | PEN | Ignores this much of the target's DEF when dealing physical damage. |
| **Life Steal** | LS | Heals the attacker for this percentage of damage dealt (capped at 50%). |

The first 4 stats (HP, ATK, DEF, SPD) appear on nearly all equipment. The other 6 stats appear on mid-to-late-game equipment and create build specialization opportunities.

### Equipment Tiers

| Tier | Region | Power Level |
|------|--------|-------------|
| T1 | Base / Starter | Basic gear (Rusty Sword, Leather Vest) |
| T2 | Forest Haven / Fungalmire | Forged and regional gear (Iron Sword, Chainmail) |
| T3 | Sunken Strand / Ashen Horizons | Specialized regional gear (Coral Blade, Magma Plate) |
| T4 | Starfall Expanse / Necropolis | High-tier gear (Prism Blade, Bone Staff) |
| T5 | Final Realm | Endgame gear (Void Edge, Rift Staff) |

Higher-tier equipment has dramatically higher stat budgets. A T1 weapon averages about 4 total stat points; a T5 weapon averages about 30.

### Consumables (59 items)

Consumables are single-use items carried in hero bags:

- **Healing** -- Tonics and bandages that restore HP
- **Food** -- Meals that provide combat buffs (cooked by the Chef)
- **Potions** -- Elixirs that boost ATK, DEF, SPD, or provide special effects
- **Thrown** -- Venom flasks, smoke bombs, and other offensive items
- **Utility** -- Salves, oils, and tactical items

### Materials (71 items)

Materials are crafting ingredients gathered from dungeons:

- **Gathering drops** -- Herbs, mushrooms, berries, raw meat, wood
- **Monster drops** -- Fangs, pelts, silk, bones, gel
- **Regional materials** -- Unique to each region (Spore Clusters, Sea Salt Crystals, Ember Dust)
- **Boss trophies** -- Rare drops from defeating region bosses, required for T4 bench crafting

### Class Books (15 items)

Class Books unlock new hero classes at the Training Hall. Each book corresponds to one class. Find them as dungeon loot or buy them at shops in the appropriate region.

## Dungeon Runs

Dungeons are where your heroes earn their keep -- or die trying.

### Entering a Dungeon

1. Make sure you have heroes in your party (at least 1, up to your party cap).
2. Visit the **Dungeon Board** facility.
3. Select a starting floor. Floor 1 is always available; higher floors unlock as you complete them.
4. Launch the run.

### How Runs Work

A dungeon run progresses room by room through a floor. At each **camp** (rest stop between rooms), you choose between two options:

- **Choice A** -- Always a combat encounter
- **Choice B** -- Either an event encounter or an elite combat encounter

There is no option to flee from the camp screen. Once you enter a dungeon, you must fight forward or extract after completing a floor.

After completing all rooms on a floor, you can **descend** to the next floor or **extract** with your loot.

### Room Types

| Type | What Happens |
|------|-------------|
| **Combat** | Fight a group of monsters. Defeat them to earn loot and gold. |
| **Elite** | A tougher combat encounter with better rewards. |
| **Event** | A choice-based encounter (e.g., loot a satchel, bargain with a fae, investigate a shrine). Each event presents choices with weighted random outcomes -- some choices are safer, others are riskier but offer better rewards. |
| **Camp** | Rest stop between rooms where you choose your next room and manage inventory. |
| **Boss** | A powerful regional boss at the end of the dungeon. Defeating it unlocks the next region. |

### Stash Rules

This is important -- pay attention to how loot works:

- Your **town stash** (Storage) is **locked** during a dungeon run. You cannot access it.
- Loot collected during a run goes into a **dungeon stash** (provisional).
- At camp, you manually route loot into individual **hero bags** (no auto-sort, no stacking in bags).
- When you **extract** (successfully leave the dungeon), your dungeon stash transfers permanently to your town stash, and gold is banked.
- **Fleeing** only happens mid-combat when a hero dies. If you flee combat, surviving heroes drop ALL equipment and bag items. You lose all unbanked dungeon loot and gold.
- If your entire party is wiped out, those heroes are gone permanently and you lose dungeon loot.

### Extraction

Extraction is how you safely end a run. After completing a floor, the camp screen offers an **Extract** button. Extraction:

- Transfers all dungeon gold to your permanent wallet
- Moves all dungeon items to your town stash (which stacks items)
- Returns you to town with all surviving heroes

## Combat

Combat is turn-based. Speed (SPD) determines turn order. Heroes and monsters with very high speed get extra actions per round: 40+ SPD = 2 actions, 80+ SPD = 3 actions (max). This makes SPD one of the most impactful stats in late-game.

### Turn Flow

1. All combatants (heroes and enemies) are sorted by SPD into a turn queue.
2. On each unit's turn, they use an ability (A or B) based on availability and cooldowns.
3. The target rolls **Evasion** -- if successful, the attack misses entirely ("EVADE").
4. If the attack hits, **Crit Chance** rolls for 1.5x damage. **Armor Penetration** ignores some target DEF.
5. Damage is calculated as: `base_power + (ATK * scaling) - (target DEF - armor_pen)`. Fire/dark/void attacks use **Resist** instead of DEF.
6. After damage, **Thorns** retaliates on the attacker and **Life Steal** heals the attacker.
7. Status effects tick at the start or end of turns.
8. Combat ends when all enemies are defeated or all heroes are dead.

### Abilities

Every hero has two abilities:

- **Ability A** (primary) -- Lower cooldown, used frequently. Often a single-target attack or heal.
- **Ability B** (special) -- Higher cooldown, bigger impact. AoE damage, buffs, debuffs, or powerful heals.

When both abilities are on cooldown, the hero uses a basic attack.

### Targeting and Rows

Heroes and monsters are arranged in a front row and back row. **Melee attackers** can only target the front row and prefer the enemy with the lowest absolute HP. **Ranged and mage attackers** can target any row and prefer the enemy with the lowest percentage HP. Position your tankier heroes in front to shield squishier damage dealers and healers.

### Monster Abilities

Monsters are not limited to basic attacks. Each monster has a **combat role** (melee, ranged, or mage) and up to 2 special abilities. Monster AI varies by difficulty tier:

- **Feral** (Region 1 basics): Only use basic attacks
- **Basic**: Use abilities in a fixed priority order
- **Tactical**: Randomly select from available abilities (unpredictable)
- **Strategic** (bosses): Use powerful abilities on rotation

Higher-region monsters use more dangerous abilities like Ground Slam (AoE), Devastating Charge (stun), Life Siphon (damage + self-heal), and Meteor (AoE + burning).

### Status Effects

**Debuffs (Damage over Time)**

| Effect | What It Does |
|--------|-------------|
| Bleeding | Physical damage each turn, stacks up to 3 |
| Burning | Fire damage each turn, stacks up to 5 |
| Poisoned | Poison damage each turn, stacks up to 3 |
| Shocked | Lightning damage + SPD reduction, stacks up to 3 |
| Stunned | Skip next turn |
| Blinded | 50% miss chance for 1 turn |

**Buffs**

| Effect | What It Does |
|--------|-------------|
| Regenerating | Heals HP each turn |
| Evasive | 50% dodge chance for 1 turn |
| Reflecting | Reflects 50% of incoming damage |
| Taunting | Forces enemies to target this hero |

All debuffs are cleansable. Some healer abilities (like Cleansing Wave) remove debuffs from allies.

### Permadeath

Heroes can die permanently in combat. If a hero's HP reaches 0, they are dead. There is no resurrection (except the Lich's Phylactery passive, which triggers once per combat). Plan accordingly -- bring healing, equip defensive gear, and do not send under-equipped heroes into tough fights.

## Crafting

Crafting happens at the town's production and equipment facilities.

### Equipment Crafting (Blacksmith, Huntsman, Enchanter)

These facilities let you:

- **Unlock recipes** by spending materials. Once unlocked, that item appears in the General Store.
- **Craft equipment** directly using materials from your stash.
- **Upgrade equipment** to higher tiers using regional materials.

Recipes are organized by tier and equipment slot. Higher-tier recipes require:
- A base item
- Regional materials specific to that region
- For T4+ items: a **boss trophy** from the region's boss

### Production Crafting (Chef, Alchemist)

The Chef and Alchemist use a **mixing system**:

1. Place 2-3 ingredients into the mixing slots.
2. Attempt the mix. If the combination is valid, you get the result and the recipe is permanently discovered.
3. If the combination is invalid, you lose the ingredients. At the Alchemist, failed mixes escalate a mishap streak that can temporarily lock facilities.

Discovered recipes are remembered permanently, so you only need to experiment once.

### Facility Tiers

Each crafting facility has a tier that can be upgraded. Higher facility tiers unlock access to higher-tier recipes and more powerful items.

## Progression

The game is structured around 7 regions, each with a unique theme, monsters, materials, and challenges.

### Region Order

| # | Region | Theme | Boss |
|---|--------|-------|------|
| 1 | **Forest Haven** (Thornhaven) | Ancient forest, natural magic | The Thorn-Ent |
| 2 | **The Fungalmire** | Bioluminescent fungal bogs | The Spiral Mycelium |
| 3 | **The Sunken Strand** | Fog-soaked coastal ruins | The Tide Sovereign |
| 4 | **Ashen Horizons** | Scorched sands, volcanic ridges | The Cinder Monarch |
| 5 | **Starfall Expanse** | Crystal groves, fractured reality | The Shattered Oracle |
| 6 | **The Necropolis** | Bone, grave-dust, forbidden magic | The Ossuary King |
| 7 | **Final Realm** | Collapsing reality, void fissures | The Prime Corruptor |

### How to Unlock the Next Region

1. Clear all dungeon floors in the current region.
2. Defeat the region boss.
3. The next region unlocks, and you receive a notification in the Town Hub.
4. Travel to the new region using the region buttons in the nav rail.

### What Each New Region Brings

- **New town** with its own facilities, NPCs, and recipes
- **New races** available for recruitment
- **New classes** available via Class Books
- **New materials** for crafting region-specific gear
- **New monsters** with unique abilities and loot tables
- **Higher-tier equipment** recipes

### Campaign Story

As you progress through the regions, narrative dialogs appear at key moments -- arriving at a new region for the first time, defeating a boss, visiting the Herald, or discovering dungeon camps. These tell the story of the corruption spreading across the land and your role in fighting it back.

### Town Destruction

The corruption fights back. Completing certain regions triggers destructive events:

| Trigger | Effect |
|---------|--------|
| Complete Region 3 | A Region 1 town resets to Tier 1 |
| Complete Region 4 | A Region 2 town is reduced to Tier 2 |
| Complete Region 5 | A Region 3 town is reduced to Tier 3 |

Towns remain functional after a tier-down -- they just lose access to higher-tier recipes until rebuilt.

## Tips for New Players

1. **Recruit a full party of 4 heroes before your first dungeon run.** A solo hero will struggle against most encounters. A Vanguard + DPS + Healer trio (or quad with a second DPS) is a solid starting composition.

2. **Equip backpacks.** Backpacks increase a hero's personal bag capacity, letting them carry more loot out of the dungeon. Even the basic Small Backpack helps.

3. **Extract early rather than die.** A successful extraction with modest loot is always better than a total party wipe. You keep everything on extract; you lose everything on death.

4. **Bring a healer.** The Warden is your first healing class. Having at least one hero who can restore HP makes a huge difference in longer dungeon runs.

5. **Check your equipment tiers.** If you are struggling in a region, you are probably under-geared. Upgrade your weapons and armor at crafting facilities before trying again.

6. **Experiment with mixing at the Chef and Alchemist.** Discovery-based crafting can yield powerful consumables. Start with common ingredients to learn basic recipes before using rare materials.

7. **Use Class Books at the Training Hall.** New classes dramatically expand your tactical options. The Striker is great for damage, but a Stormcaller or Ashblade from later regions hits much harder.

8. **Manage your party composition.** Balance your team across archetypes. A party of all DPS will hit hard but crumble under pressure. A tank (Vanguard) draws enemy fire while your damage dealers work.

9. **Position your rows carefully.** Put tanky Vanguards in the front row to absorb melee attacks. Keep your healers and ranged DPS in the back -- but remember that ranged and mage enemies can still reach them.

10. **Invest in Speed.** SPD determines not just turn order but also how many actions a hero gets per round. A hero with 40+ SPD acts twice, and 80+ SPD acts three times. Speed rings and amulets pay for themselves quickly.
