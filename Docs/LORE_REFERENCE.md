# Shops & Shadows — Lore & World Reference

> **Purpose:** A complete summary of all existing game lore, world-building, races, classes, monsters, towns, regions, and narrative context. Use this as a reference for writing quests, dialogue, event text, and campaign content.

---

## Game Identity

**Title:** Shops & Shadows
**Genre:** Single-player cozy grim-fantasy roguelite
**Tone:** Cozy grim-fantasy — warm lantern-lit town interiors contrasted with ominous corrupted forests and ruins. The world is dangerous but not hopeless. Humor and charm exist in hero personalities, townfolk, and events, even while stakes remain high.

**Core Fantasy:** You are the quiet force behind every adventure. Heroes come and go — some die ignobly in forgotten tunnels, some survive and grow into legends. The greatest eventually retire into permanent town roles (blacksmiths, guards, trainers). You, the Shopkeeper, recruit expendable heroes, equip them from your curated inventory, send them into corrupted regions, and use the resources they bring back to grow your town.

**You are not the warrior — you empower them.**

---

## Design Pillars

1. **Town Growth <-> Hero Power <-> Region Cleanse** — Stronger towns produce better gear; better gear lets heroes cleanse deeper corruption; cleansed regions unlock more town growth.
2. **Persistent Progression with Disposable Heroes** — Heroes are expendable, but their contributions matter. Deaths feed the Book of the Dead; successes can promote them into Legacy roles.
3. **Item-Centric Progression** — Resource -> Facility -> Crafted Gear -> Shop -> Heroes -> Adventure -> More Resource.
4. **Exploration as Risk/Reward Routing** — Map choices matter. Players pick routes through combat, events, resources, and corruption risk.

## World Structure

The world is divided into **7 major regions**, each with a unique biome, corruption flavor, unique resources, legendary materials, and a regional boss.

### Region Unlock Sequence (Linear)

1. Forest Haven (starter)
2. The Fungalmire
3. The Sunken Strand -> **Town Destruction Event begins**
4. Ashen Horizons
5. Starfall Expanse
6. The Necropolis
7. Final Realm (endgame)

## Town Destruction Timeline

The corruption fights back. Completing later regions triggers devastating setbacks:

| Trigger | Effect |
|---------|--------|
| Complete Region 3 | A random Region 1 town resets all buildings to Tier 1 |
| Complete Region 4 | A random Region 2 town is reduced to Tier 2 baseline |
| Complete Region 5 | A random Region 3 town is reduced to Tier 3 baseline |

Towns remain rebuildable and functional after tier-down. Regular failed defenses tier down buildings by 1 but never below Tier 1.

## Corruption System

Corruption is the central antagonistic force. Each region has a distinct corruption flavor:

- **Region 1:** Dormant Nature Corruption
- **Region 2:** Spore Corruption
- **Region 3:** (Unspecified — tidal/coastal)
- **Region 4:** Flame Corruption
- **Region 5:** Arcane Corruption
- **Region 6:** Death Corruption
- **Region 7:** Full Corruption (void fissures, collapsing reality)

Corruption manifests through:
- Enemy behavior and abilities
- Environmental tile mechanics in dungeons
- Town destruction events triggered by regional completion
- Boss encounter mechanics

## Key Narrative Themes

1. **Sacrifice and Legacy** — Heroes are expendable; their deaths feed the Book of the Dead and fuel progression. The Shopkeeper's role is bittersweet.
2. **Rebuilding Against Entropy** — Corruption degrades what you build. The town destruction mechanic means progress is never permanent.
3. **Empowerment Through Others** — You never fight directly. Your power is expressed through the heroes you equip and the town you build.
4. **Cozy vs. Grim** — The tension between safe, warm town life and the dangerous corrupted wilds is central to the game's identity.

---

## Glossary of Key Terms

- **Shopkeeper** — The player character. Manages town, equips heroes, never fights directly.
- **Legacy Hero** — A retired hero who takes a permanent town role (blacksmith, guard, trainer).
- **Book of the Dead** — A record of all fallen heroes, contributing to persistent progression.
- **Corruption** — The spreading darkness that threatens all regions. Each region has a unique corruption flavor.
- **Regional Boon** — Passive bonuses granted when both towns in a region are operational.
- **Tier-Down** — Town destruction mechanic that reduces building tiers in earlier regions.
- **Extraction** — Successfully returning from a dungeon run with gathered resources.
- **Stash** — Global shared storage for materials across all towns.
- **Backpack** — Hero-specific inventory with limited capacity (no stacking in bags).
