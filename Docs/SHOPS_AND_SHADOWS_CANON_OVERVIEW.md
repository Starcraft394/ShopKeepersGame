# SHOPS & SHADOWS — CANON OVERVIEW

**Version:** 1.1
**Status:** Locked Canon (Foundational)
**Purpose:** Defines the metaphysical, political, timeline, and corruption rules of the world.
This document governs story, systems, NG+, faction design, and endgame structure.

---

## 1. GAME IDENTITY

**Title:** Shops & Shadows
**Genre:** Cozy Grim-Fantasy Roguelite with Multi-Timeline Progression
**Core Fantasy:** You are the Shopkeeper — the quiet architect of resistance.
You do not fight. You build.

The world is warm inside town walls and dangerous beyond them.
Humor exists. Death exists. Regret exists.

---

## 2. COSMOLOGY & METAPHYSICAL RULES

### 2.1 The Grail of Life

The Grail is an ancient temporal artifact capable of rewriting history.

- It does not rewind time.
- It **edits** it.

**Rules:**
- Requires massive void-temporal pressure to activate.
- Can only manifest at the Region 7 Nexus.
- Causes a full historical rewrite.
- Void-exposed entities retain memory.
- Cannot be triggered repeatedly without renewed void concentration.

> The Grail is not inherently evil. It is unstable.

### 2.2 Altered Void Power

Void power is not darkness — it is raw temporal manipulation energy.

When altered or amplified unnaturally:
- It **emits**.
- Like radiation.

This emission is known as: **Corruption**

### 2.3 The Anchor Stone (Fixed Temporal Object)

The Anchor Stone is the original void meteor fragment from the first Grail rupture.

**Properties:**
- Persists across all timeline rewrites.
- Immune to historical editing.
- Exists outside world-origin chronology (extra-world matter).
- Radiates void corruption.
- Evolves visually with accumulated void pressure.
- Serves as the ultimate nexus point for endgame conflict.

**Spatial Behavior:**
Each rewrite reshuffles geography around the Anchor. The Anchor remains spatially fixed — the world rearranges around it. The starting region always forms near it.

**Timeline Evolution:**

| Timeline | Anchor State |
|----------|-------------|
| 1 | Subtle glow |
| 2 | Surface fractures deepen |
| 3 | Oozing begins earlier; minor distortions visible from start |
| 4+ | Reacts to retained heroes; emits pulses during regional boss defeats |

**Endgame Rotation:**
Void spreads from the Anchor outward each timeline. One region becomes dominant early; that region's leader gains access to Anchor influence and discovers Grail rewrite potential — becoming the endgame boss. The Void Sovereign was simply the first to reach it. Subsequent leaders are not serving void; they are exploiting it.

> The Anchor remembers. The Shopkeeper remembers.

**Shopkeeper Awareness Arc:**
- Timeline 1: No awareness. Anchor is background detail.
- Timeline 2: Déjà vu. Subtle recognition. Unease near stone.
- Timeline 3: Realization — "It was always there. It is growing faster. It does not reset."

> The mystery turns into dread. The player connects it. No exposition — just implication.

## 3. CORRUPTION SYSTEM (CANON)

**Corruption = Void Radiation.**

**Properties:**
- Spreads from high-exposure hosts.
- Mutates ecosystems based on region.
- Enhances strength and volatility.
- Leaves crystalline residue.
- Persists across rewrites in concentrated forms.

**Each region expresses corruption differently:**

| Region | Corruption Expression |
|--------|----------------------|
| Nature | Accelerated growth |
| Spore | Replication |
| Tidal | Environmental instability |
| Fire | Volatile amplification |
| Arcane | Reality fracture |
| Death | Soul destabilization |
| Void | Raw emission |

Bosses are high-density emitters.

## 4. POLITICAL HISTORY

### 4.1 The King

- Injured in his father's war.
- Unable to produce an heir.
- Inherited a weary kingdom.
- Obsessed with correcting succession.
- Funded Grail research.
- Overrode researcher advisement.
- Forced unsafe void amplification.
- Caused temporal rupture.
- Shut down project.
- Buried records.
- Declared void heretical.
- Now denies corruption to avoid admitting origin.

> He is regret-driven, not tyrannical.

### 4.2 The Grand Strategist -> Void Sovereign

- Former royal tactician.
- Lost his lover in the rupture.
- Remembers due to direct void exposure.
- Believes the King is unfit.
- Seeks Grail to rewrite succession.
- Weaponizes corruption to recreate conditions for manifestation.

> He is grief-driven, not chaotic.

## 5. THE SHOPKEEPER

The player character.

- Not royal.
- Not arcane elite.
- Pragmatic.
- Infrastructure-focused.

**The Shopkeeper always remembers rewrites.**

However:
- They do not understand the cause until late Region 7 (first timeline).
- Their resistance comes from long-term exposure to void crystals and crafted stabilizers.

They represent the third ideology:
> **Build forward, not rewrite backward.**

## 6. MEMORY RETENTION SYSTEM

### 6.1 Fractured Grail Shard

- Dropped by first endgame boss.
- Used to craft: **Memory Sigil**

### 6.2 Memory Sigil

An equippable item. A hero with a Memory Sigil equipped survives the rewrite and carries over into NG+.

- The Shopkeeper always remembers (automatic — no sigil needed).
- Any hero with a Memory Sigil equipped is retained through the rewrite.
- No hero limit — if you can craft enough sigils, you can carry your whole roster.

### 6.3 Timeline Cores

Each NG+ final boss drops a **Timeline Core** (region-specific).

Used to craft additional Memory Sigils. More timelines completed = more sigils available = more heroes retained.

**Hero Retention Rules:**
- Retained heroes keep: race, class, all equipped gear, bag contents.
- Retained heroes **lose levels** — they reset to level 1 (the rewrite reshapes their experience, not their identity).
- Non-retained heroes are lost entirely in the rewrite.

**Goal:** Carry enough retained heroes to permanently sever the rewrite cycle.

## 7. NG+ TIMELINE SYSTEM

Each timeline:
- Corruption originates in a different region.
- **The player starts in that region's starting town.** This defines the available starting classes and races.
- That region's faction leader becomes the NEW endgame boss.
- Their corruption type becomes globally dominant.
- Enemy variants reflect dominant corruption.
- Strategy requirements shift.
- **Items roll higher stats in NG+** — quality tiers scale with timeline progression.

**Starting Region Defines Your Build:**

Each NG+ rewrite places the Shopkeeper in a new region. The classes and races native to that region become your starting options. You must build from a fundamentally different roster each timeline.

| Timeline Origin | Starting Classes | Starting Races |
|----------------|-----------------|----------------|
| Nature (base) | Defender, Striker, Warden, Druid | Human, Elf, Dwarf, Mossfolk |
| Spore | Fungal Berserker + neutrals | Mossfolk + neutrals |
| Tidal | Tidechaser, Stormcaller + neutrals | Tidelings + neutrals |
| Fire | Pyrewarden, Ashblade + neutrals | Dragonkin + neutrals |
| Arcane | Prism Sentinel, Prism Lancer + neutrals | Crystalborn + neutrals |
| Death | Dark Channeler, Lich + neutrals | Undead + neutrals |
| Void | Voidwalker, Void Herald + neutrals | Voidwalkers + neutrals |

**Examples:**

**Fire-Origin Timeline:**
- Start in Fire region town with Pyrewarden/Ashblade/Dragonkin.
- Fire enemies buffed.
- Player fire damage reduced.
- Ember variants across regions.
- Environmental heat hazards.
- Items roll higher quality tiers.

**Fungal-Origin Timeline:**
- Start in Spore region with Fungal Berserker/Mossfolk.
- Regeneration heavy.
- Spread mechanics.
- Infection tiles.

**Death-Origin Timeline:**
- Start in Death region with Dark Channeler/Lich/Undead.
- Undead variants everywhere.
- Resurrection mechanics.
- Soul economy intensified.

> NG+ increases difficulty through strategic inversion AND roster restriction, not just stat inflation.

Endgame bosses drop: **Timeline Shards**

- Research fragments.
- Suppressed transcripts.
- Memory echoes.
- Political revelations.
- Personal journals.

> Lore is earned, not dumped.

## 9. ENDGAME STRUCTURE

- **Base Timeline Final Boss:** Void Sovereign.
- **Subsequent NG+:** Regional faction leader becomes final boss.

Rewrite triggers when:
- Grail reaches critical void concentration.
- Endgame boss initiates emergency rollback.

**Final meta-goal:**
Accumulate enough retained heroes to prevent further rewrites permanently.

- Color grading shift.
- Environmental mutation.
- NPC visual adaptation.

## 12. RULES THAT CANNOT BE BROKEN

1. The Shopkeeper always remembers.
2. Void-exposed entities retain memory.
3. Corruption spreads from usage, not morality.
4. The Grail requires void pressure.
5. Every rewrite increases instability.
6. No rewrite is identical.
7. Progression must always feel earned.
8. The Anchor Stone persists across all rewrites — it is extra-world matter and cannot be edited.
9. The starting region always forms near the Anchor Stone.
