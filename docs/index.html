Core Features

Town management meets tactical combat meets extraction roguelite

Your Shop, Your Strategy

Build and upgrade 10 facility types across multiple frontier towns

⚔

Town Management

Build and upgrade 10 facility types: Inn, Shop, Storage, Training Hall, Blacksmith, Huntsman, Enchanter, Alchemist, Chef, and Dungeon Board. Each tier unlocks better services, recipes, and recruits.

♟

Hero Recruitment

Recruit heroes from 9 races and 15 classes. Each hero has unique stat growth, 2 abilities, 2 class passives, and a racial passive. Heroes who die are gone forever.

🗡

Equipment System

430 items across 5 tiers with 10 combat stats. 8 equipment slots per hero including weapon, armor, offhand, and a backpack for extra dungeon carry capacity.

🌄

Dungeon Exploration

Room-by-room roguelite dungeon runs with choice-based pathing. Risk combat, events, or elite encounters. Extract to bank loot permanently, or push deeper and risk losing everything.

⚙

Crafting & Discovery

Equipment crafting at Blacksmith, Huntsman, and Enchanter. Discovery-based mixing at Chef and Alchemist. Regional materials and boss trophies unlock high-tier recipes.

🔂

New Game Plus

9 NG+ cycles with escalating difficulty, hero carry limits, ability infusion, permanent stat bonuses, and a true ending at Cycle 8 when the Anchor Stone shatters.

A Story About Regret, Grief, and Choosing to Build Forward

Sent to the frontier town of Thornhaven by the Crown, you discover a world ravaged by void corruption — the consequence of a grief-stricken King's desperate gamble with an ancient temporal artifact called the Grail of Life.

"A shopkeeper. The Crown sends a shopkeeper to fight corruption. How... economical."
— The Void Sovereign

As you push deeper through seven corrupted regions, the truth emerges: the King caused the rupture. A former royal tactician named Valdric, who lost his lover Lira in the disaster, now wages war as the Void Sovereign to seize the Grail and rewrite history itself.

The question at the heart of every timeline: Is erasure of suffering worth the erasure of everything built in response to it?

New Game Plus

9 cycles of escalating challenge, culminating in the True Ending

The Anchor Stone Remembers

Each cycle rewrites the timeline. The corruption adapts. Only you remember.

Hero Carry

Carry 1 hero into NG+1, scaling up to all heroes by NG+4. Build your legacy team across timelines.

Difficulty Scaling

Monster HP scales from 1.0x to 2.5x. ATK/DEF from 1.0x to 1.8x. Speed from 1.0x to 1.2x. Every cycle demands sharper tactics.

Ability Infusion

Infuse learned abilities into equipment slots, letting heroes carry techniques across timelines.

Permanent Bonuses

+5% base stats per cycle from NG+5 onward. Equipment gains bonus stat lines based on completions.

Unique Dialogs

New campaign dialogs in NG+ cycles reveal hidden truths. Characters remember fragments across rewrites.

True Ending

At Cycle 8, the Anchor Stone shatters. The rewrite cycle ends permanently. The corruption finally recedes.
