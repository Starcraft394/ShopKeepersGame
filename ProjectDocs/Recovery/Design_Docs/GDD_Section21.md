------------------------------------------------------------
📘 SECTION 21 — POSTGAME & NEW CYCLE (NG+) SYSTEM
------------------------------------------------------------

This section defines the hybrid postgame progression that activates after the player completes the full 7-region campaign. It describes carryover heroes, world resets, corruption scaling, the World Tome meta system, resource carryover, facility worker XP, and early-cycle town defense.

21.1 Campaign Completion → New Cycle Transition

When the player completes the final boss of Region 7:

✔ The world resets

All towns, facilities, dungeons, and region locks revert to their initial T1/T2 states.

✔ The player retains:

A carryover party (5 heroes initially; increases in future cycles)

Their gear

Their traits, affinities, and hero levels

A selection of Prestige Materials (see 21.5)

Cosmetic “Memorial Hero” data

✔ Everything else resets:

Town facility tiers

Dungeon tiers

Shop state

Regular hero roster

Book of the Dead (archived and restarted)

This begins Cycle 2, which is harder and more corrupted than Cycle 1.

21.2 Carryover Party System

At the end of each campaign:

✔ The player chooses 5 heroes to carry forward

These heroes:

Keep all gear

Keep their level

Keep passives, affinities, traits

Can be assigned to facilities immediately

Can fight immediately

Act as the backbone of early NG+ progression

✔ Future cycles increase carryover limit:

Cycle 2: 5

Cycle 3: 6

Cycle 4: 7

Cycle 5+: 8 (cap)

This rewards long-term roster development and multiple-cycle mastery.

21.3 Memorial Heroes (Book of the Dead Preservation)

When the Book of the Dead is archived:

✔ Player selects up to 5 fallen heroes as “Memorial Heroes.”

These provide:

Cosmetic appearance entries in future hire pools

Only appear if their race is active in the Housing Facility

No stat bonuses

No passive buffs

Pure world-building, emotional continuity, and hero identity flavor

21.4 Early-Cycle Town Defense Activation

In Cycle 1 (first campaign), town defense begins after Region 3.

In NG+ (postgame cycles):

✔ Town Defense is activated after Region 1

Attack Chance: 15% whenever the player returns from a dungeon

Only Region 1 towns are attacked at first

Buildings degrade by 1 tier per failed defense (never below T1)

Heroes cannot die in town defense

Successful defense generates Town Treasury gold

Treasury gold is shared across the region

This provides early-cycle hero leveling, gold flow, and strategic resource pressure.

21.5 Prestige Material Carryover

At campaign completion, the player may carry:

10 Basic Materials

5 Rare Materials

1 Epic/Legendary Material

These allow early facility upgrades in NG+ without trivializing progression.

Rules:

Only player-chosen materials are carried

They go into a “Cycle Vault” accessible from any town

Vault materials cannot exceed these limits

Legendary material can be used only for facility upgrades or legendary crafting

21.6 Shared Global Storage

In all cycles:

✔ Town Storage is unified

No per-town or per-region storage.

This dramatically reduces UI complexity and ensures resource planning is centralized.

21.7 Housing Facility — Race Unlock Rules in NG+

During Campaign 1:

Races unlock region-by-region.

In NG+:

All previously unlocked races remain globally unlocked forever.

Housing Facility tiers only determine how many race slots appear, not which races are available.

Example:

Tier 1 Housing → Any 3 unlocked races
Tier 4 Housing → All races unlocked in the game can appear

This prevents bottlenecks with postgame carryover heroes.

21.8 Corruption & Tile Hazards in NG+

In postgame cycles:

✔ Corruption is active from Region 1
✔ Tile hazards appear immediately
✔ Elite room frequency is increased
✔ Dungeon scaling begins one region earlier

The postgame begins “hard mode” from the very start.

21.9 The World Tome (Meta Progression System)

After completing a campaign, the World Tome unlocks.

Structure:

3 Pages: Heroes, Facilities, Dungeons & Events

Each page: 5 nodes

Each node: 3 ranks

Each rank: 5 Tome Points

One special node (Shop Rarity Floor): 10 Tome Points per rank

Total cost to fill the World Tome = 225 Tome Points
21.9.1 HEROES PAGE

Node 1 — Hero ATK Boost
+15% → +30% → +45%

Node 2 — Hero Max HP Boost
+5% → +10% → +20%

Node 3 — XP Gain
+10% → +20% → +35%

Node 4 — Crit Chance
+3% → +6% → +10%

Node 5 — Resistances
+3% → +6% → +10%

21.9.2 FACILITIES PAGE

Node 1 — Crafting Quality (Shop Output)
+6% → +12% → +20%

Node 2 — Facility Upgrade Cost Reduction
-12% → -24% → -36%

Node 3 — Shop Rarity Floor (Prestige Node)
Rank 1: Minimum Uncommon
Rank 2: Minimum Rare
Rank 3: Minimum Rare+

Node 4 — Shop Slot Expansion
+1 → +2 → +3 slots

🔥 Node 5 — Worker XP from Production (Final Version)

Rank 1 — Unlock Production XP
Workers gain XP each time their facility produces items.
Formula:

XP = (Items Produced × 5)


Rank 2 — +50% XP
Items × 5 × 1.5

Rank 3 — +100% XP
Items × 5 × 2.0

This makes facility workers meaningful and endgame-relevant.

21.10 Dungeons & Events Page

Node 1 — Elite Rewards Improved
+3% → +7% → +12%

Node 2 — Resource Yield Boost
+5% → +10% → +15%

Node 3 — Corruption Hazard Resistance
-4% → -8% → -12%

Node 4 — Better Event Rewards
+5% → +10% → +20%

Node 5 — Boss Material Rarity Increase
+3% → +6% → +10%

21.11 Final NG+ Loop

Complete all regions and final boss

Choose 5 carryover heroes

Select 5 Memorial Heroes for cosmetic continuity

Bring 10 basic + 5 rare + 1 epic/legendary material

Earn World Tome Points and invest in upgrades

Towns reset → facilities reset

Begin new cycle with corruption active

Town Defense begins after Region 1

Housing has all races available

Continue campaign with higher difficulty

Beat final boss again → unlock additional carryover slots and more Tome Points

Repeat for ascending cycles

This creates a powerful, compelling, replayable prestige structure.
