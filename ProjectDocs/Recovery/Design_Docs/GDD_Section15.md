15. RACES & CLASSES FRAMEWORK

This section defines the racial and class systems that shape hero identity, progression, and combat roles within Shops & Shadows.
It also establishes how races and classes are discovered region-by-region and how they interact with town development, hero hiring, and dungeon progression.

All systems described here are data-driven, region-bound, and designed for long-term scalability.

15.1 SYSTEM GOALS

Player Agency:
Races and classes are player-driven unlocks chosen during region progression.

Clarity & Identity:
Each race and class has distinct thematic and mechanical roles without overwhelming complexity.

Thematic Regional Progression:
New races/classes are introduced in regions whose environments match their thematic identity.

Adaptive Party Composition:
At each region milestone, players gain:

One new race

Two new classes (one magic/healer, one melee/tank/rogue)

Non-Restrictive Synergy:
Races do not restrict class options.
Instead, races offer small bonuses that can synergize with any class without dictating builds.

Hero Training Economy:
Class books appear in the shop, not facilities (exception: Lich).
Class assignment becomes a strategic investment.

15.3 STARTER RACES (REGION 1)
15.3.1 Human

Identity: Flexible, adaptable, cooperative.
Stat Profile: Balanced HP, ATK, SPD, and DEF.
Racial Trait — Versatile Learner: +10% XP earned (combat + facility).
Town Affinity: +2% worker speed in any facility.
Notes: The “safe choice” race. Strong early game presence.

15.3.2 Elf

Identity: Agile, perceptive, lightly magical.
Stat Profile: High SPD & Accuracy; low base HP.
Racial Trait — Keen Sight: Reveals +1 node into map fog-of-war IF at least one Elf is in party.
Town Affinity: Works well in precision facilities: Woodsman, Jeweler, Alchemist.
Notes: Early scouting specialists.

15.3.3 Dwarf

Identity: Durable, stubborn, metal/stone aligned.
Stat Profile: High HP & DEF; low SPD.
Racial Trait — Deep Miner: +1 extra ore roll; small chance to upgrade one ore tier.
Town Affinity: Significant bonus in Blacksmith and metal-based facilities.
Notes: Ideal frontline and crafting synergy.

15.4 MIDGAME RACES (REGIONS 2–5)
15.4.1 Mossfolk (Region 2)

Biome: Swamp / Fungal Forest
Identity: Regenerative, spore-born, semi-plant humanoids.
Stat Profile: Moderate HP, strong regen, low DEF, good RES.
Racial Trait — Sporebond:

+2% regen per turn

Weak poison resistance

Extra herbal resource find chance in Nature dungeons
Town Affinity: Herbalist, Chef, Alchemist
Notes: Perfect synergy with Region 2 classes.

15.4.2 Tidelings (Region 3)

Biome: Flooded ruins, marshlands, coasts
Identity: Amphibious waterfolk, highly adaptive
Stat Profile: Good SPD, moderate HP, high debuff resistance
Racial Trait — Amphibious:

Immune to “Wet” penalties

Gains +SPD or +Dodge on wet tiles

Resistant to Shock effects
Town Affinity: Fishing/food-related facilities, Alchemist
Notes: Tidelings thrive in tile manipulation combat.

15.5 LATE-GAME RACE (REGION 6)
Undead

Biome: Necropolis, Deathlands
Identity: Returned souls, strange sustain mechanics
Stat Profile: High resistances, moderate HP, inconsistent SPD
Racial Trait — Deathless:

Immune to poison, bleed, sleep

Reduced healing from food

Heals when causing corruption or life-drain
Town Affinity: Necropolis Forge, Graveyard Defenses
Notes: Must be carefully balanced; high reward, medium risk.

15.10 HERO PROGRESSION & TRAINING
1. Early Game (Very First Dungeon)

Heroes have no classes

Only Basic Attack

No abilities

Tutorial dungeon teaches combat fundamentals

2. After returning from first dungeon

Player builds:

Storage

Training Hall

Blacksmith

Leatherworker

Basic facilities (Tier 1)

Training Hall now sells starter class books.

3. Assigning Classes

The first class assignment is a major identity moment.

Costs gold + sometimes resources

Class determines:

Stat curve

Gear access

Abilities

Role type

4. Region-Based Class Unlocks

Once in a new region:

Town A unlocks one race

Town B unlocks two new classes

Both class books appear in shop thereafter

15.11 HERO HIRING RULES

Heroes appear at Housing as:

Race only

Level 1–3

No class

Basic worn gear

Class selection is entirely player-driven via Training Hall.

Late-game Housing can attract:

Higher-level heroes

Heroes with unique traits

Special roles (if desired later)

15.12 RACE + CLASS NON-RESTRICTIVE SYNERGY

No race restricts any class.

Instead:

Small synergy bonuses

Thematic combinations feel good

Weird/off-meta builds remain viable

Examples:

Elf Ranger: +1 Accuracy

Dwarf Warrior: +1 Armor

Beastkin Rogue: +1 Crit

Mossfolk Sporeweaver: +1 Regen

Tidelings Tempest Caller: +2% Shock Resist

Undead Dark Channeler: +Corruption resist

These are small, flavorful, non-punishing bonuses.

15.14 TOWN EXPANSION & STARTER TOWN KIT

Only Town 1 starts empty.

Before moving to a new region, players must gather enough materials to build the Starter Town Kit, which auto-constructs in any new town:

Training Hall (T1)

Blacksmith (T1)

Leatherworker (T1)

Alchemist (T1)

Storage

Housing (T1)

Shop (fed by facilities)

This ensures:

No rebuild grind

Clean pacing

Regions feel “fair” and ready

Town 1 is destroyed after Region 3, then becomes rebuildable with the same kit, plus new expansions.
