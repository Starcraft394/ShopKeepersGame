16. REGIONS OVERVIEW

The world of Shops & Shadows is divided into seven major regions, each representing a thematic biome, a stage of progression, and a narrative escalation in the creeping corruption threatening the land.
Every region contains:

Two towns (except Regions 1 & 7)

A Race Unlock Town (Town A)

A Class Unlock Town (Town B)

Unique biome elements

Distinct enemy families

Region-specific resources

A regional boon (combat + gathering)

A boss encounter

A corruption type (dormant early, active late-game)

Regions escalate in complexity, danger, and strategic depth while maintaining the cozy-grim tone of the game.

------------------------------------------------------------
🌲 REGION 1 — FOREST HAVEN
------------------------------------------------------------
Biome Theme:

A vast ancient forest network, woven with natural magic and protective root-barriers.
Lush, calm, and cozy — the perfect setting for the start of the adventure.

Town A — Greenroot Village (Starter Town)

A nurturing, nature-bound settlement built atop a tangle of massive living roots.

Homes formed from branch-weaving

Mud-and-bark living walls

Natural root fortifications

Calm, rustic vibe

First heroes recruited here (Human, Elf, Dwarf)

Function:
✔ Tutorial town
✔ Introduces Race System
✔ Town starts with no buildings, must be constructed manually
✔ The home of your earliest heroes

Town B — Timberfall (Starter Class Town)

A thriving woodland frontier where civilization begins to take shape.

Wood-and-stone structures

Early metalwork

The first Training Hall

Symbolizes unity between the 3 starter races

Function:
✔ Unlocks first 5 core starter classes:
Warrior, Ranger, Mage, Rogue, Acolyte
✔ Introduces crafting via basic facilities
✔ Starts the player’s strategic resource loop

Enemy Families:

Forest Wolves

Vinebound Horrors

Spriggan Tricksters

Moss Trolls

Ancient Root Golems

Boss Concept: The Thorn-Ent
A corrupted guardian of the forest whose defeat unlocks Region 2.

Regional Boon (When Both Towns Are Active):

Combat: +X% Max HP
Gathering: +X% Wood yield
(X = total building levels across both towns)

Corruption Type:

Dormant Nature Corruption
Appears only in late/postgame as creeping root-blights.

🍄 REGION 2 — THE FUNGALMIRE
------------------------------------------------------------
Biome Theme:

A deep fungal forest full of spores, bioluminescence, and two distinct mushroom cultures — one healthy, one psychedelic and strange.

Town A — SproutRest (Race Unlock: Mossfolk)

A serene, spore-softened mushroom village.

Healthy mushrooms along floors and trees

Soft bioluminescent spores

Mossfolk homeland

Calm and nurturing

Function:
✔ Unlocks Mossfolk as a playable race
✔ Introduces herbal resources

Town B — Magic Caps Rest (Class Unlock: Sporeweaver + Grove Guardian)

A towering psychedelic fungal metropolis.

Giant mushroom-tree

Spiraling, multicolored caps

Slightly eerie, mystical atmosphere

Function:
✔ Unlocks:

Sporeweaver (magic/healer hybrid)

Grove Guardian (tank/support hybrid)
✔ Enhances introduction to debuffs, regen, spores, root-based tanking

Enemy Families:

Fungal Shamblers

Spore Wisps

Rotcap Brutes

Hallucinogenic Myconids

Bloom Giants

Boss Concept: The Spiral Mycelium
A psychic fungal superorganism.

Regional Boon (When Both Towns Are Active):

Combat: +X% Regeneration
Gathering: +X% Herb yield

Corruption Type:

Spore Corruption
Dormant for now, becomes active in late game or postgame.

🌊 REGION 3 — THE SUNKEN STRAND
------------------------------------------------------------

A fog-soaked coastal expanse of ruins, shifting tides, and amphibious life.
Waterlogged forests, kelp-choked shallows, and ghostly waves fill the landscape.

Town A — Shelldrift Harbor (Race Unlock: Tidelings)

A coastal village built on wooden platforms above tidal pools.

Seashell decor and luminescent algae

Amphibious architecture

Gentle nautical atmosphere

Function:
✔ Unlocks Tidelings race
✔ Introduces wet tiles, tide mechanics, shock interactions

Town B — Mistwhisper Shoals (Class Unlock: Tempest Caller + Harpooner)

A mystical fog-laden settlement built among coastal ruins.

Echoing waves

Pale blue fog

Storm magic everywhere

Function:
✔ Unlocks classes:

Tempest Caller (water/lightning mage)

Harpooner (mid-range melee disruptor)
✔ Introduces tile manipulation and pull/push combat mechanics

Enemy Families:

Tidewalkers

Storm Wisps

Crustacean Brutes

Drowned Spirits

Mistborn Leviathans

Boss Concept: The Tide Sovereign
A massive amphibious tyrant who commands storms.

Regional Boon:

Combat: +X% Dodge / Evasion
Gathering: +X% Fishing yield

⚠️ Critical Event: Town 1 Destruction

At the end of Region 3:

A random town in Region 1 is destroyed

All buildings reset to Tier 1

Town defense system begins

Region 2 towns now face attacks after expeditions

This marks the transition to the midgame.

🛡️ TOWN DEFENSE SYSTEM (Integrated Across Regions)
Begins after Region 3
Attacks roll after every expedition
Towns that lose:

One random building tiers down by 1

Defense Formation:

Mixed grid with defensive and offensive tiles

Heroes fight together

Tile bonuses scale with facility tier

World Escalation:

After Region 3: Region 1 town destroyed

After Region 4: Region 2 town destroyed

After Region 5: Region 3 town destroyed

Stronger mobs attack higher regions
