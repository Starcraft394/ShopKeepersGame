------------------------------------------------------------
📘 SECTION 18 — RESOURCE & FACILITY INPUT SYSTEM (FINAL VERSION)
------------------------------------------------------------

Resources in Shops & Shadows serve two core functions:

Upgrading town facilities

Fueling those facilities to automatically craft randomized gear for the Shopkeeper’s shop

The player does not manually craft items — instead they gather materials, deliver them to facilities, and decide which facility slots appear in the shop. Each facility then generates gear based on:

The materials supplied

The region the town is in

The facility type

The facility tier

The region’s passive pool

This keeps gameplay simple, strategic, thematic, and focused on resource management rather than crafting menus.

18.3 Facility Item Generation (Autocrafting)
--------------------------------------------

Facilities do not produce items instantly — they generate a batch of shop stock after each expedition.

How item generation works:

Player selects facility slots for the shop (ex: 2 Blacksmith, 1 Leatherworker).

Facility consumes:

Basic materials

Region materials

(Optional) specialty materials

Facility crafts 1 random item per slot.

Item pulls from:

That facility’s item list

That region’s passive pool

Tier matching the material quality

Higher-tier materials = better items and stronger passive rolls.

This system:

Creates region-identity in gear

Makes farming meaningful

Keeps itemization fresh

Avoids tedious crafting menus

Healing

Resistances

Temporary boosts

Health flasks are ALWAYS craftable.

--------------------------------------------
18.6 Field Artificer Resource Privilege
--------------------------------------------
The Field Artificer has special carry rules:

They can retain 1–2 special materials

AND retain 1–2 crafted items

BUT only if they return to town alive

Their kept items do not enter town storage

Their personal items persist until replaced or destroyed

If they die → everything is lost as normal

This makes Field Artificer a strategic late-game class.

--------------------------------------------
18.7 Insurance Rules
--------------------------------------------

Uninsured materials → lost on retreat or party wipe

Insured materials → kept

Legendary materials require late-game insurance slots

Gold inheritance follows Region 3+ rules

Field Artificer exception applies as above
